AI Development & Automation FAQ.

How much does an AI chatbot cost?

It depends on where it runs (website, WhatsApp or both), what it needs to know and which systems it connects to, such as bookings or a CRM. We scope a focused first version and give a clear quote after a short call.

Can AI automate my business?

AI can automate repetitive, rules-based work very well: answering common questions, reading documents, sorting enquiries and moving data between tools. We start by finding the tasks where it saves the most time, and keep people in charge of the judgement calls.

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

We build assistants that answer from your own approved information, show sources where useful, and hand off to a person when they're unsure. We test them on real questions before launch.

Can you build a WhatsApp chatbot for my business?

Yes. We build WhatsApp chatbots using the official WhatsApp Business API, so customers can ask questions, book or order right inside WhatsApp.

Is our data safe?

We only use the data needed for the task, keep it in secure, access-controlled systems and agree data handling with you up front.
