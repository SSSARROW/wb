Custom Software Development FAQ.

How much does custom software development cost?

It depends on scope: the number of user roles, screens, integrations and how much automation is involved. We give a clear, clear quote after a short discovery call, and we usually suggest a focused first release so you see value early and spend on what matters.

How long does it take to build custom software?

A focused first version is typically measured in weeks, not months. We agree the timeline during discovery, then build in short sprints so you see working software throughout, not just at the end.

Can we outsource software development to you from abroad?

Yes. Most of our clients are outside Sri Lanka, where our team is based. We work across time zones over email, WhatsApp and video calls, with regular progress updates.

What happens after launch?

We stay on to monitor, fix and improve the software, and keep adding features as your processes change.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Usually, yes. Most projects involve connecting to tools you already use, such as spreadsheets, CRMs, accounting or payment systems, rather than replacing everything at once.
