UI/UX Design FAQ.

What does a UI/UX designer do?

UX design shapes how a product works: the flows, structure and decisions a user makes. UI design shapes how it looks and feels: layout, type, colour and components. We do both, then hand over designs that are ready to build.

How much do UI/UX design services cost?

It depends on the number of screens and flows, whether research and prototyping are needed, and whether you need a design system. We quote after a short call about your goals.

Can you redesign our existing app or website?

Yes. We start with a UX audit of what's working and what isn't, then redesign in stages so you keep momentum and don't lose what users already like.

Do you also build what you design?

Yes. Our designers and developers work as one team, so designs are built exactly as intended, which is one of the main reasons clients choose us.

Do you design for accessibility?

Yes. We design with readable contrast, clear focus states and sensible structure so products work for more people.
