Mobile App Development FAQ.

How much does it cost to build an app?

App development cost depends on the features (accounts, payments, bookings, chat), the number of screens and the backend behind them. We share a clear quote after a short discovery call, and often recommend a focused first release to keep the initial budget lean.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP is much faster than a feature-complete product. We agree a realistic timeline during discovery and build in sprints, so you can test the app on your own phone as it takes shape.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We build cross-platform apps, usually with React Native, so one codebase runs on both iPhone and Android. That keeps development and maintenance costs down.

Should I build a web app or a mobile app?

If your customers use it often, need notifications or want it on their home screen, a mobile app is usually right. If it's mostly used at a desk or occasionally, a web app can be faster and cheaper. We'll recommend the best fit during discovery.

Will you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listings, handle submission and review, and ship updates after launch.
