      Questions, answered.

      What businesses usually ask before working with a software development company.

      What does SenXDev do?

      SenXDev is a custom software development company. We design and build mobile apps, web apps, websites and AI solutions for startups and businesses worldwide, and support them after launch.

      Where is your team, and do you work with international clients?

      Our team is based in Sri Lanka, and we work with clients in the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia and beyond. We work across time zones over email, WhatsApp and video calls, with regular progress updates.

      Why outsource software development to an offshore team?

      An offshore team gives you experienced design and engineering without the cost and time of hiring in-house. You get one accountable team for discovery, design, build and support, with clear communication throughout.

      How much does a project cost?

      Every project is scoped individually. Tell us what's slowing your business down, and after a short discovery call we'll send a clear, clear quote, often for a focused first release.

      How long does it take to build an app or website?

      It depends on scope. We agree a realistic timeline during discovery and build in short sprints, so you see working progress throughout the project.

      What services do you offer?

      Custom software development, mobile app development for iOS and Android, web app development, website development, AI chatbots and automation, and UI/UX design.

      Do you offer support after launch?

      Yes. We monitor, fix and improve what we build, and keep adding features as your business grows.
