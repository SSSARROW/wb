3 automation ideas for retail shops

Sound familiar?

Stock checks, customer messages about availability and keeping sales numbers up to date.

#1 Start here

"Is it in stock?" answered automatically

Customers ask on WhatsApp or Instagram whether you have an item, and get an answer from your live stock list.

How it would work

  1. Stock levels are read from your POS (if it allows outside connections) or a Google Sheet you keep up to date.
  2. A customer messages asking about a product or size.
  3. An assistant checks the stock list and replies with what's available.
  4. They can ask you to hold it, and you get a notification.
Takes off your plate
Checking the shelves or stock room for every availability message.
Setup time
about 2 weeks

#2

Low-stock alerts before you run out

You get a WhatsApp message with a ready-made reorder list when items drop below the level you choose.

How it would work

  1. Each item's minimum stock level and supplier are set in a simple sheet.
  2. Sales from your POS update the stock count.
  3. When items drop below their level, you get a WhatsApp alert with a reorder list grouped by supplier.
  4. If your POS already sends basic low-stock alerts, we build on those rather than repeat them.
Takes off your plate
Counting stock and noticing too late that something has run out.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

A daily sales summary on your phone

Every evening you get a short summary of the day's sales, best sellers and anything unusual.

How it would work

  1. At closing time, the day's sales are pulled from your POS.
  2. They're added to a Google Sheet so you have a running history.
  3. You get a short WhatsApp summary with totals and top items.
Takes off your plate
Pulling reports from the POS and updating spreadsheets by hand.
Setup time
about 1 week
