3 automation ideas for coaches & training centres

Sound familiar?

Answering course questions, booking calls and keeping students on track.

#1 Start here

Answer course questions on Instagram

An assistant answers questions about your courses, dates and how to join, and books discovery calls.

How it would work

  1. We give the assistant your course details and the questions you usually get.
  2. It replies to DMs on Instagram and WhatsApp straight away.
  3. Interested people are offered a time for a call from your calendar.
Takes off your plate
Answering the same course questions in DMs every day.
Setup time
about 1 week

#2

Automatic student onboarding

When someone signs up, they get their welcome message, materials and calendar invites automatically.

How it would work

  1. A new sign-up comes in through your payment page or form.
  2. They get a welcome message on WhatsApp or email with their materials.
  3. Session invites are added to their calendar.
  4. Their details are added to your student sheet.
Takes off your plate
Sending welcome messages, links and invites by hand.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

Weekly student check-ins

Students get regular check-in messages, and you see who might need a hand.

How it would work

  1. Each week, students who signed up for check-ins get a short message asking how they're doing.
  2. Their replies are collected in one sheet.
  3. An AI step highlights anyone who sounds stuck, so you can reach out.
Takes off your plate
Checking on every student one by one.
Setup time
about 1 week
