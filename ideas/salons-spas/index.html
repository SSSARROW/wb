3 automation ideas for salons & spas

Sound familiar?

Booking appointments over DMs, no-shows and reminding clients to come back.

#1 Start here

Book appointments straight from Instagram DMs

Clients who message on Instagram or WhatsApp can pick a service and a free slot without waiting for you to reply.

How it would work

  1. A client messages asking for an appointment.
  2. An AI assistant asks which service they want and which day suits them.
  3. It checks your Google Calendar, or your booking app if it allows outside connections, and offers the free slots.
  4. The appointment is booked and the client gets a confirmation message.
  5. If your booking app doesn't allow it, the assistant sends your booking link instead.
Takes off your plate
Going back and forth in DMs to find a time that works.
Setup time
about 2–3 weeks

#2

Appointment reminders with one-tap confirm

Clients get a WhatsApp reminder before their appointment and can confirm or reschedule with one reply.

How it would work

  1. The day before an appointment, the client gets a WhatsApp reminder.
  2. They reply to confirm, or ask to move it.
  3. Reschedule requests are offered new slots from your calendar.
  4. Your calendar updates and your team sees who has confirmed.
Takes off your plate
Calling or texting every client the day before.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

Bring regulars back on time

Clients who are due their next visit get a personal message inviting them to book again.

How it would work

  1. Each client's last visit and service are kept in a simple sheet or your booking system.
  2. When they're due again, for example six weeks after a colour, a message is drafted for them.
  3. It's sent on WhatsApp with a link to book, only to clients who agreed to hear from you.
Takes off your plate
Working out who is due back and messaging them yourself.
Setup time
about 1 week

Setup times cover building and testing. If your WhatsApp number isn't set up for automation yet, Meta's approval can add a few days to a couple of weeks.
