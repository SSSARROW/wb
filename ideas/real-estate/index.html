3 automation ideas for real estate agencies

Sound familiar?

Replying to property enquiries fast, qualifying leads and booking viewings.

#1 Start here

Reply to every enquiry in minutes

New enquiries from portals, Instagram and your website get a friendly reply and a few questions straight away.

How it would work

  1. An enquiry arrives from a property portal's lead email, Instagram or your website form.
  2. The lead gets a WhatsApp message within minutes asking about budget, area and timing.
  3. Their answers are added to your CRM or Google Sheet.
  4. The right agent is notified with a short summary.
Takes off your plate
Copying enquiries out of emails and chasing basic details.
Setup time
about 1–2 weeks
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#2

Book viewings without the phone tag

Interested leads pick a viewing slot from the agent's calendar on WhatsApp.

How it would work

  1. When a lead is ready to view, they're sent the free viewing slots.
  2. They pick one and it's added to the agent's Google Calendar.
  3. Both sides get a reminder before the viewing with the address.
Takes off your plate
Calling back and forth to agree viewing times.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

New listings sent to matching buyers

When you add a listing, buyers whose needs match it get a message about it.

How it would work

  1. You add a new listing to your sheet or CRM as usual.
  2. It's matched against saved buyer requirements, such as area, budget and bedrooms.
  3. Matching buyers who asked to hear about new listings get a short WhatsApp or email message with the details.
Takes off your plate
Going through your contacts to find who might want a new listing.
Setup time
about 1–2 weeks

Setup times cover building and testing. If your WhatsApp number isn't set up for automation yet, Meta's approval can add a few days to a couple of weeks.
