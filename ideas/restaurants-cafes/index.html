3 automation ideas for restaurants & cafes

Sound familiar?

Answering the same questions on WhatsApp and Instagram, taking bookings by hand and chasing reviews.

#1 Start here

WhatsApp bookings without the back-and-forth

Guests book a table on WhatsApp and get an instant confirmation, while you see every booking in one list.

How it would work

  1. A guest messages your WhatsApp Business number asking for a table.
  2. An AI assistant asks for the date, time, number of people and name.
  3. It checks your booking sheet, or your reservation system if it allows outside connections, and offers free times.
  4. Once they confirm, the booking is saved and they get a reminder on the day.
  5. Large groups and special requests are passed to your team.
Takes off your plate
Replying to every booking message and writing tables into the book by hand.
Setup time
about 2 weeks

#2

Instant answers to common questions

An assistant answers questions about your menu, hours, parking and dietary options on WhatsApp and Instagram.

How it would work

  1. We give the assistant your menu, opening hours and the answers you already give every day.
  2. When someone messages on Instagram or WhatsApp, it replies straight away.
  3. Anything it isn't sure about, such as large events or complaints, is passed to your team.
Takes off your plate
Typing the same answers about hours, menu and location all day.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

A review request after every visit

Guests who booked get a friendly thank-you message the next day with a link to leave a Google review.

How it would work

  1. After a booking's date has passed, the guest is picked up from your booking list.
  2. They get a short WhatsApp message thanking them for coming, with a link to your Google review page.
  3. Every guest gets the same link, as Google's rules require.
  4. Anyone who replies with a problem is flagged to you so you can follow up personally.
Takes off your plate
Remembering to ask for reviews and following up with guests one by one.
Setup time
about 1 week

Setup times cover building and testing. If your WhatsApp number isn't set up for automation yet, Meta's approval can add a few days to a couple of weeks.
