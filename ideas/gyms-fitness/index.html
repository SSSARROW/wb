3 automation ideas for gyms & fitness studios

Sound familiar?

Trial sign-ups, class bookings and members who quietly stop coming.

#1 Start here

Follow up every trial sign-up

People who ask about a trial get booked in and followed up automatically.

How it would work

  1. Someone fills in your trial form or messages on Instagram.
  2. They get a WhatsApp message to pick a time for their first session.
  3. After the trial, they get a friendly check-in and your membership options.
  4. Your team sees everyone's status in one Google Sheet.
Takes off your plate
Chasing trial leads and remembering who to follow up.
Setup time
about 1–2 weeks

#2

Class bookings and waitlists on WhatsApp

Members book classes by message, and waitlisted members are told the moment a spot opens.

How it would work

  1. A member messages to book a class.
  2. The assistant checks your class schedule, in a sheet or in your gym software if it allows outside connections.
  3. If there's space they're booked in, and if not, they join the waitlist.
  4. When someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist gets a message.
Takes off your plate
Managing class lists and waitlists by hand.
Setup time
about 2–3 weeks
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

Check in with members who stop coming

Members who haven't visited for a while get a personal message from your team.

How it would work

  1. Visit dates come from your check-in system or a sheet.
  2. Each week, members who haven't been in for a set time are listed.
  3. A friendly message is drafted for each, and you approve before it's sent on WhatsApp.
Takes off your plate
Spotting quiet members and writing to them one by one.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you

Setup times cover building and testing. If your WhatsApp number isn't set up for automation yet, Meta's approval can add a few days to a couple of weeks.
