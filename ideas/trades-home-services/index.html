3 automation ideas for trades & home services

Sound familiar?

Quote requests, scheduling jobs and chasing unpaid invoices.

#1 Start here

Collect job details before you quote

Customers who ask for a quote are asked for the details and photos you need, before you call them back.

How it would work

  1. A customer messages on WhatsApp or fills in your website form.
  2. They're asked for the job type, address and a few photos.
  3. Everything is saved to a Google Sheet, with the photos in a linked Drive folder.
  4. You get one tidy summary, ready for you to quote.
Takes off your plate
Calling customers back just to get the basic details.
Setup time
about 1 week

#2

Book jobs and send reminders

Customers pick a time from your calendar and get a reminder the day before you arrive.

How it would work

  1. Once a quote is accepted, the customer gets a link to pick from the slots you've opened, with travel time left between jobs.
  2. The job goes into your Google Calendar with the address and notes.
  3. The customer gets a WhatsApp reminder the day before.
Takes off your plate
Scheduling jobs over the phone and remembering to remind people.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you
#3

Friendly invoice reminders

Unpaid invoices get polite WhatsApp reminders with a payment link, on a schedule you choose.

How it would work

  1. Invoices are read from your accounting tool, such as Xero or QuickBooks, or a sheet.
  2. When one passes its due date, the customer gets a polite WhatsApp reminder with the invoice and payment link.
  3. This adds WhatsApp to the email reminders your accounting tool may already send.
  4. A second reminder goes out later if needed, and you're told who is still unpaid.
Takes off your plate
Chasing payments yourself.
Setup time
about 1 week
Set this up for my business → 15-min call to plan it with you

Setup times cover building and testing. If your WhatsApp number isn't set up for automation yet, Meta's approval can add a few days to a couple of weeks.
